How NSX routes between virtual and physical networks. Much of this was revision from the NSX install and configure course.

Goals

  • Describe logical routing between virtual and physical networks
  • Tell Tier-0 and Tier-1 gateways apart
  • Explain single-tier and multi-tier topologies, logically and physically
  • Identify NSX dynamic routing and ECMP capabilities

What I covered

  • The roles of Tier-0, Tier-1 and edge clusters.
  • Single-tier versus multi-tier routing designs.
  • Uplink, downlink and the automatically created router-link interfaces.
  • BGP, OSPF, route redistribution and ECMP.

Lessons learned

  • Tier-0 provides upstream connectivity and supports dynamic routing and ECMP.
  • NSX Manager creates the intra-tier transit and router-link interfaces automatically.
  • Tier-1 gateways connect to Tier-0 through router links, never directly to other Tier-1s.
  • NSX ECMP supports up to eight paths and uses five-tuple hashing.
