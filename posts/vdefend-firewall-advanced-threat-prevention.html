Key takeaways

  • IDS/IPS inspects in two places: at each VM (distributed) and on the Tier-1s (gateway). The same attack can show up in both.
  • Start in Detect Only, read the events, then move to Detect & Prevent.
  • A custom profile with packet capture leaves a PCAP for every hit.
  • Virtual patching: a small profile for one CVE, in prevent mode, under Emergency Threat Rules.
  • IDS/IPS finds known attacks, NTA finds odd behaviour, and NDR ties both into one campaign.
