The fundamentals of stretching a VCF cluster across two availability zones.

Goals

  • Understand the fundamentals of multi-AZ VCF stretched clusters

What I covered

  • Host counts.
  • Witness placement.
  • Latency.
  • Bandwidth.
  • Traffic separation examples.

Lessons learned

  • Stretch the management domain before the workload domains.
  • Put the vSAN witness in a third, independent failure domain.
  • Use redundant switches and uplinks, and separate storage traffic where possible.
  • Size inter-site bandwidth for guest VM I/O and replication.
