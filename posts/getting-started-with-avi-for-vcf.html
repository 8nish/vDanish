Module 5: VCF Automation All Apps

The All Apps org is self-service for VMs and containers, with vSphere Supervisor underneath. AKO (the Avi Kubernetes Operator) does the load balancing part. You ask for a Kubernetes load balancer or gateway and Avi builds it, without ever opening the Avi UI.

Key takeaways

  • Avi controllers line up with NSX Managers, 1:1 or 1:N.
  • The controller is the brain, the Service Engines do the load balancing. The cloud connector lets the controller place SEs by itself.
  • An SE group sets how many SEs, how big, the HA mode and where they go.
  • Organization Managed SE groups belong to one org, Provider Managed ones are shared.
  • In an All Apps org I never had to open Avi to get a load balancer. A load balancer on the VM, or a Gateway and HTTPRoute, was enough.
