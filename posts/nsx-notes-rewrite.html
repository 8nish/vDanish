Two days of rewriting rough NSX notes into something I can come back to.

Goals

  • Strengthen NSX knowledge

What I covered

  • Uplink profiles, and the logical switching packet walk with Geneve.
  • Controller tables: VNI to TEP, VM MAC to TEP, and VM MAC to VM IP.
  • Logical routing in the logical and physical view; Tier-0 versus Tier-1; single and multi-tier.
  • Edges, how gateways are realized as DR and SR, and gateway interfaces.
  • BGP and OSPF in NSX, and failover with BFD and routing timers.

Lessons learned

  • The best way to remember something is to rewrite the notes.
