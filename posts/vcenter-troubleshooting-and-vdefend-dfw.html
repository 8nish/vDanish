A vCenter refresher in the morning, the building blocks of the vDefend firewall after that.

Goals

  • Strengthen vCenter troubleshooting
  • Cover vDefend topics such as the Distributed and Gateway Firewall

What I covered

  • vMotion, HA and DRS, and common issues like ESX not responding to vCenter.
  • Added remediations to my NSX east-west and north-south troubleshooting flowchart.
  • Firewall concepts: policies, rules, groups, services and context profiles.
  • Policy settings (TCP strict, locked, stateful), time-based policies and identity firewall.
  • Blocking known malicious IPs with the built-in threat feed.

Lessons learned

  • The order of the DFW policy categories (below).
  • Context profile attributes such as App ID, custom URL and FQDN.
  • A DFW troubleshooting flowchart with remediations.

DFW categories, in order

  1. Ethernet
  2. Emergency
  3. Infrastructure
  4. Environment
  5. Application
