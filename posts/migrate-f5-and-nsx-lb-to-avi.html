Key takeaways

  • ACT moves an F5 config or the NSX load balancer to Avi. Nothing gets created until the playbook is pushed.
  • iRules become Avi objects: HTTP policy sets, DataScripts or network security policies.
  • The version in the wizard has to match what the controller runs.
  • A patch has to use the names of your own Avi setup, like the VRF and the network.
  • Every playbook comes with a _delete one, and cutover is separate from clean up. Both give you a way back.
