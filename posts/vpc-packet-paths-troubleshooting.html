Following packets through a VPC, then writing a flowchart for when they don't arrive.

Goals

  • Trace east-west and north-south packet paths
  • Build a VPC routing troubleshooting flowchart

What I covered

  • Distributed and centralized VPC topologies.
  • Host-based routing versus edge-based routing.
  • Checked subnets, gateways, VNIs and connectivity settings.
  • Worked through DHCP and external-access failures.

Lessons learned

  • East-west routing decisions happen at the source VPC gateway.
  • Scenario: an unconnected subnet gateway and a disabled DHCP server left a VM without a usable address.
  • External access may need NAT and correct route redistribution.
