VPCs give each team its own self-service network space. This lab builds them from two places, vCenter and VCF Automation, and puts workloads on them.

What I did

  • Created VPCs from vCenter and from VCF Automation.
  • Added private and public subnets.
  • Attached workloads to those subnets and checked connectivity.

Notes worth keeping

  • VPCs in different projects are isolated unless you explicitly connect them.
  • Private subnets need NAT or an external IP to reach the outside; public subnets don't.
  • A distributed transit gateway needs the same external VLAN available on every ESX host.
  • East-west routing decisions happen at the source VPC gateway.
