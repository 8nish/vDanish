Lessons learned

  • The Controller is the control plane; Service Engines (SEs) are the data plane.
  • SEs keep forwarding traffic in headless mode if the Controller cluster fails.
  • Production should use a three-node Controller cluster.
  • Licensing is based on SE vCPU, not on the number of Controllers.
  • Service Engine Groups define sizing, HA, scaling and placement.
  • Built-in end-to-end timing, request logs and topology views make troubleshooting easier.
  • VCF Operations is the preferred deployment path.
  • Coming from F5 or NetScaler, most concepts carry over.
