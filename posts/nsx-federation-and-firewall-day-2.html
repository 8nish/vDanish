Multi-site NSX with Federation, plus a firewall troubleshooting flowchart and day-2 operations.

Goals

  • Strengthen Federation and multi-site concepts
  • Build a DFW and Gateway Firewall troubleshooting flowchart
  • Cover day-2 operations of firewalls

What I covered

  • Global Manager clusters, active and standby in two locations.
  • Sync: GM to LM through the async replicator, and LM to LM through APH.
  • Objects created on the GM are read-only on LMs; ownership can move from LM to GM.
  • RTT recommendations, Tier-0 and Tier-1 span, and Tier-0 primary and secondary locations.
  • RTEPs on edge nodes. Started URL filtering and FQDN analysis, to revisit.

Lessons learned

  • RTEP supports fragmentation and TEP doesn't, so RTEP works at 1500 MTU (though it isn't recommended).
  • The packet walk from location A to location B.
  • Do most of the configuration on the Global Manager, not the Local Managers.
