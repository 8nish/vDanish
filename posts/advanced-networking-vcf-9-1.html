A hands-on tour of multi-site networking in VCF 9.1 with NSX Federation, from onboarding a second site to running workloads on segments that span both locations.

What I did

  • Onboarded a second NSX Manager from the Global Manager.
  • Configured BGP on the Tier-0 gateway and used a route map to control what gets advertised.
  • Configured RTEPs on the edge nodes so overlay traffic can cross between sites.
  • Moved workloads in vCenter from distributed port groups to stretched NSX segments.

Notes worth keeping

  • Objects created on the Global Manager show up read-only on the Local Managers, so do the configuration on the GM.
  • RTEPs are the inter-site tunnel endpoints on the edges, separate from the TEPs used inside a site.
