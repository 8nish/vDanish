First HCX day: the pieces, what each site needs, and how the two sides get paired.

Goals

  • Identify HCX Cloud, Connector and service mesh components
  • Explain source and destination site requirements
  • Describe HCX deployment and site pairing

What I covered

  • HCX management and data plane components.
  • Deploying HCX Cloud at the destination first, then the Connector at the source.
  • Unidirectional versus bidirectional site pairing.
  • Troubleshooting checks for deployment and pairing.

Lessons learned

  • The destination needs NSX; the source doesn't.
  • A service mesh groups the paired appliances and services used for migration.
  • HCX uses OS Assisted Migration for non-vSphere workloads such as KVM and Hyper-V.
